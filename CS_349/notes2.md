# Professor's Notes

Throughout engineering history, certainty has gradually become less absolute.

Classical software engineering asked

> Is the program correct?

Expert systems asked

> How confident are we?

Modern Artificial Intelligence asks

> Why should we trust this conclusion?

The next generation of intelligent systems may ask an even more difficult
question

> Why should I trust what I currently believe?

That question lies beyond the scope of this course.

It becomes the starting point of CS451.
