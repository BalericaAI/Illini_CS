# Professor's Notes

Throughout the history of computing, engineers have traditionally focused upon
correctness.

Modern enterprise AI introduces a different engineering challenge.

A system may execute correctly while reasoning from knowledge that is
incomplete, contradictory, obsolete, or insufficiently trusted.

Students should therefore continually ask

> "Why should this system believe what it currently believes?"

This question extends beyond software correctness.

It becomes one of the defining engineering questions of trustworthy
intelligent systems.
