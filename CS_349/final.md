CS349 Final Exam

University of Illinois
December 1998

Problem 7 (40 points)

Two autonomous underwriting systems possess identical computational capability, identical inference engines, and identical processing speed.

They nevertheless reach opposite conclusions.

One approves the policy.

One rejects it.

You have verified:

    No software defects exist.
    No hardware faults exist.
    No communication failures occurred.
    Both systems executed correctly.

Explain the most likely engineering cause.

Support your answer using concepts from CS341, CS342, CS346, and CS347.

Finally answer the following question.

If two equally intelligent systems reach different conclusions, is the problem their intelligence... or what they chose to trust?

There is no single correct answer.

There are, however, poorly defended answers.
