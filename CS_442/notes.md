# Professor's Notes

Students frequently describe neural networks as systems that "learn."

This statement should immediately provoke another question.

> "What exactly has been learned?"

Throughout this course students should distinguish

- parameters,
- representations,
- knowledge,
- reasoning,
- understanding,
- and trust.

Although neural networks frequently achieve remarkable performance,
performance alone does not explain the internal representations responsible
for their decisions.

Understanding those representations remains one of the central challenges of
modern Artificial Intelligence.
