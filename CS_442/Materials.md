# Recommended Books

Artificial Neural Networks represent one of several approaches to constructing
intelligent systems.

Unlike Expert Systems, which encode explicit knowledge, neural networks
construct internal representations through experience.

Students should understand not only how neural networks operate, but why
connectionist approaches emerged, disappeared, and later returned to become
one of the dominant paradigms of Artificial Intelligence.

---

# I. Historical Foundations

---

## Donald Hebb

### The Organization of Behavior

1949

Role

The origin of Hebbian Learning.

Importance

Introduces the principle

> "Cells that fire together wire together."

Modern Connection

One of the conceptual foundations of neural learning.

---

## Frank Rosenblatt

### Principles of Neurodynamics

Role

The Perceptron

Importance

Introduces one of the earliest trainable neural models.

Students should understand why Rosenblatt's work generated enormous optimism
during the 1960s.

---

## Marvin Minsky
## Seymour Papert

### Perceptrons

Role

Historical Turning Point

Importance

Explains the limitations of early perceptrons.

Students should understand why neural network research slowed during the
first AI Winter.

---

# II. Classical Connectionism

---

## Rumelhart
## McClelland

### Parallel Distributed Processing
Volumes I & II

Role

Primary Historical Text

Importance

Perhaps the most influential work in modern connectionism.

Students study

- distributed representations
- learning
- internal representations
- cognition

Relationship

Preparation for CS448.

---

## James McClelland

Selected Papers

Importance

Understanding distributed knowledge.

---

# III. Core Engineering Texts

---

## Simon Haykin

### Neural Networks

Role

Primary Course Text

Importance

Comprehensive engineering treatment of

- perceptrons
- multilayer networks
- backpropagation
- recurrent networks
- optimization

Students should derive algorithms rather than merely use software libraries.

---

## Christopher Bishop

### Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning

Role

Probabilistic Perspective

Importance

Shows the relationship between statistical learning and neural networks.

Students compare neural methods with classical pattern recognition.

---

## Michael Nielsen

### Neural Networks and Deep Learning

Role

Modern Companion

Importance

One of the clearest explanations of backpropagation ever written.

Excellent laboratory companion.

Available freely online.

# Suggested Reading Order

1. Hebb
2. Rosenblatt
3. Minsky & Papert
4. Rumelhart & McClelland
5. Haykin
6. Bishop
7. Nielsen
8. Goodfellow
9. Russell & Norvig

Students should observe how neural computation evolved from biological
inspiration through mathematical formalization toward modern foundation
models.

#Bonus

Karl Popper

Conjectures and Refutations

Role: Scientific Method for Engineers

Why it belongs:
Students spend the semester building hypotheses about how neural networks behave, designing experiments to challenge those hypotheses, and revising their mental models. That's Popper's philosophy in practice.
