# Professor's Notes

Many students enter this course believing that intelligent systems fail because
their algorithms are weak.

Enterprise experience suggests a different reality.

Most intelligent systems fail because the underlying knowledge is incomplete,
contradictory, obsolete, or poorly understood.

Throughout this course students should continually ask

> "How do we know this knowledge is correct?"

This question becomes increasingly important in modern AI systems that consume
large collections of documents, policies, regulations, and organizational
knowledge.

Knowledge acquisition did not disappear with the arrival of Large Language
Models.

Its tools changed.

Its importance did not.
