# Professor's Notes

Students often encounter the misconception that machine learning replaced
classical Artificial Intelligence.

History tells a different story.

Pattern recognition emerged because many real-world problems could not be
solved through logical reasoning alone.

Throughout this course students should continually ask

> "What can be learned from data?"

Immediately followed by

> "What cannot?"

The answers to these questions determine whether a problem should be solved
through

- symbolic reasoning,
- statistical learning,
- or a hybrid combination of both.

Modern enterprise AI systems increasingly combine all three approaches.
