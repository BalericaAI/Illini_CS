# Professor's Notes

Students frequently encounter claims that Artificial Intelligence underwent a
complete revolution with the arrival of machine learning.

History suggests a more nuanced picture.

Machine learning expanded the methods available to AI researchers but did not
replace symbolic reasoning, knowledge representation, or logical inference.

Throughout this course students should continually ask

> "What information can be learned from data, and what knowledge must still be
> provided by people?"

Modern enterprise AI systems frequently combine both approaches.

Understanding when learning is appropriate—and when explicit knowledge is
necessary—is one of the defining skills of an AI engineer.
