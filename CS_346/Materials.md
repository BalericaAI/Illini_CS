# Recommended Books

Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning represents the transition from
symbolic reasoning toward statistical learning.

Unlike previous courses that emphasize explicit knowledge and formal logic,
this course examines how intelligent systems discover regularities within
observational data.

The books below illustrate the historical development of statistical
Artificial Intelligence from the early 1970s through the emergence of modern
machine learning.

---

# I. Historical Foundations

These works established the mathematical foundations of statistical learning.

---

## Thomas Bayes

### An Essay Towards Solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances

Era

1763

Role

The birth of Bayesian reasoning.

Importance

Bayes introduced a mathematical framework for updating beliefs as new
evidence becomes available.

Although written centuries before computers, Bayesian reasoning eventually
became one of the foundations of pattern recognition, probabilistic
inference, and machine learning.

Modern Connection

Bayesian reasoning remains fundamental to uncertainty estimation,
classification, probabilistic graphical models, and trustworthy AI.

---

## Ronald A. Fisher

### Statistical Methods for Research Workers

Era

1925

Role

Modern statistical inference.

Importance

Fisher developed many statistical techniques still employed throughout
scientific research and machine learning.

Students should recognize Fisher's influence upon experimental design,
hypothesis testing, and statistical estimation.

---

# II. Classical Foundations

---

## Richard O. Duda & Peter E. Hart

### Pattern Classification and Scene Analysis

Role

Primary course text.

Importance

One of the most influential books ever written on pattern recognition.

Students study

- feature extraction
- classification
- decision theory
- clustering
- statistical pattern recognition

Modern Connection

Although contemporary machine learning employs dramatically larger models,
the concepts introduced by Duda and Hart remain fundamental.

---

## Nils J. Nilsson

### Principles of Artificial Intelligence

Relationship

Bridge from symbolic AI.

Importance

Students compare deterministic reasoning with probabilistic reasoning.

---

## Judea Pearl

### Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems

Role

Reasoning under uncertainty.

Importance

Introduces Bayesian Networks and probabilistic inference.

Students discover how uncertainty can be represented mathematically rather
than through deterministic rules.

Modern Connection

Bayesian reasoning continues to influence AI safety, robotics, diagnosis,
decision support, and causal inference.

---

# III. Machine Learning

---

## Tom M. Mitchell

### Machine Learning

Role

Historical bridge toward modern machine learning.

Importance

Mitchell formalizes machine learning as a scientific discipline and introduces
many concepts that continue to influence AI research.

Topics include

- concept learning
- decision trees
- version spaces
- inductive learning

Modern Connection

Many contemporary machine learning techniques evolved directly from these
foundational ideas.

---

## Christopher M. Bishop

### Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning

Role

Modern reference.

Importance

Provides a probabilistic treatment of modern machine learning.

Students should compare Bishop's statistical approach with Duda and Hart's
earlier work.

---

# IV. Advanced Reading

---

## A. Philip Dawid, Robert G. Cowell,
## Steffen L. Lauritzen

### Probabilistic Networks and Expert Systems

Relationship

Preparation for CS349.

Importance

Demonstrates how probabilistic graphical models extend classical expert
systems.

Students should compare

Production Rules

versus

Bayesian Networks

and evaluate when each approach is appropriate.

---

## Simon Haykin

### Neural Networks

Relationship

Preparation for CS442.

Importance

Introduces the mathematical foundations of artificial neural networks while
providing historical continuity from statistical learning.

---

## Rumelhart & McClelland

### Parallel Distributed Processing

Relationship

Preparation for CS442.

Importance

One of the foundational works that helped establish connectionist approaches
to Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Suggested Reading Order

Students unfamiliar with machine learning should read

1. Bayes
2. Fisher
3. Duda & Hart
4. Pearl
5. Mitchell
6. Bishop
7. Dawid, Cowell & Lauritzen
8. Haykin

The objective is to understand how statistical learning gradually emerged as
a complementary approach to symbolic Artificial Intelligence rather than as
its replacement.
