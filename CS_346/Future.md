I think this is also where I'd plant the seed for CS349.

Near the end of the semester, I'd write one sentence on the board:

"Accuracy is not trust."

Then I'd erase everything else.

Students would spend an hour arguing about it.

Some would say:

"A model with 99% accuracy should be trusted."

Then you'd quietly produce one slide:

Accuracy: 99%

Training Data:
1998–2005

Production Data:
2028

Trust?

Silence.

Exactly.

That single moment becomes the bridge into Certainty within Knowledge-Based Environments and, later, Trust and Assurance in Intelligent Systems.

I have a feeling that's the moment where your students stop thinking like machine learning practitioners and start thinking like enterprise AI engineers.
