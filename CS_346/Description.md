# CS346 - Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning
**University of Illinois (Historical Reconstruction - circa 1994)**

> "Intelligence is not merely reasoning from rules—it is recognizing patterns
> within the world."

---

# Course Description

CS346 introduces students to the mathematical and computational foundations
of pattern recognition and machine learning.

Where previous courses emphasized symbolic reasoning and knowledge
representation, CS346 explores how intelligent systems discover structure
within data through observation, classification, statistical inference, and
adaptive learning.

Students study methods for recognizing patterns, classifying observations,
estimating probabilities, and constructing predictive models. Emphasis is
placed upon understanding the mathematical principles underlying learning
rather than the implementation of specific software libraries.

The course presents machine learning as an extension of Artificial
Intelligence rather than a replacement for symbolic reasoning.

Students compare statistical learning with expert systems and investigate
where each approach succeeds or fails.

---

# Course Objectives

Upon successful completion students should be able to

- Explain the difference between symbolic AI and statistical learning.
- Describe supervised and unsupervised learning.
- Implement classical pattern recognition algorithms.
- Construct classifiers from observational data.
- Analyze classification accuracy.
- Evaluate overfitting and generalization.
- Compare probabilistic and deterministic approaches.
- Explain the historical evolution of machine learning.
- Understand the relationship between learning and reasoning.

# Learning Philosophy

Machines may reason using formal logic, but they may also discover structure
through observation.

Students should understand that pattern recognition complements symbolic
reasoning rather than replacing it.

The objective is to appreciate why different AI problems require different
computational approaches.

# Modern Perspective

Modern Artificial Intelligence frequently emphasizes deep learning and large
foundation models.

These systems remain fundamentally dependent upon concepts introduced in
classical pattern recognition, including feature representation,
classification, probability, statistical inference, and generalization.

Students completing CS346 should recognize that contemporary machine learning
is best understood as the continued evolution of decades of research in
pattern recognition and statistical decision making.
