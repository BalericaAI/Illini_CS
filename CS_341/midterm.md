CS341 Midterm — Spring 1994

Problem 7

A squirrel has developed an expert system for identifying undergraduate freshmen carrying Chick-fil-A.

Represent the squirrel's reasoning as Horn clauses.

Then determine whether the squirrel should initiate a retrieval operation.

Extra Credit:

Construct a Bayesian Network representing the probability of successful snack acquisition during the first three weeks of the Fall semester.
