# Recommended Books

Mechanized Mathematical Inference sits at the intersection of philosophy,
mathematics, computer science, and Artificial Intelligence.

Students should recognize that modern automated reasoning did not emerge from
computer science alone. Many of its central ideas originated in philosophy,
logic, and mathematics over two thousand years before the invention of the
computer.

The books below are organized according to the historical evolution of
mechanized reasoning.

---

# I. Historical Foundations

These works establish the philosophical and logical foundations upon which
Artificial Intelligence was later built.

---

## Aristotle

### Organon

Era

~350 BC

Role

The birth of formal logical reasoning.

Importance

Aristotle developed the first systematic treatment of deductive reasoning
through syllogistic logic. Although modern predicate logic eventually replaced
Aristotelian logic for most computational purposes, the fundamental idea that
reasoning follows formal rules originated here.

Students should understand Aristotle not merely as a philosopher, but as one
of the earliest architects of formal reasoning.

Modern Connection

Every inference engine ultimately owes an intellectual debt to Aristotle.

---

## George Boole

### An Investigation of the Laws of Thought

Era

1854

Role

Logic becomes algebra.

Importance

Boole transformed logical reasoning into symbolic mathematics.

Boolean algebra became one of the foundations of digital computation.

Modern Connection

Digital circuits, programming languages, database queries, and theorem proving
all depend upon Boolean logic.

---

## Gottlob Frege

### Begriffsschrift

Era

1879

Role

Birth of Predicate Logic.

Importance

Frege introduced first-order predicate logic, dramatically expanding the
expressive power of formal reasoning beyond Boolean algebra.

Modern Connection

Predicate logic remains one of the primary knowledge representation languages
used throughout Artificial Intelligence.

---

# II. Classical Foundations

---

## Alan Turing

### Computing Machinery and Intelligence

Role

The philosophical foundation of Artificial Intelligence.

Importance

Introduces the famous Turing Test while exploring whether machines can be said
to think.

Students should compare Turing's questions with modern discussions regarding
Large Language Models.

---

## J. Alan Robinson

### A Machine-Oriented Logic Based on the Resolution Principle

Role

Birth of automated theorem proving.

Importance

Introduces the Resolution Principle and Unification.

This paper transformed logic into an algorithm.

Modern Connection

Resolution remains one of the foundational techniques in symbolic reasoning.

---

# III. Core Course Texts

---

## Robert Kowalski

### Logic for Problem Solving

Role

Primary technical reference.

Importance

Introduces logical inference, theorem proving, and computational reasoning from
an engineering perspective.

Students should study Kowalski carefully before beginning PROLOG.

Modern Connection

Symbolic reasoning remains central to policy engines, business rules, and
hybrid AI systems.

---

## Patrick Henry Winston

### Artificial Intelligence

Role

Companion reference.

Importance

Provides a broader AI context within which logical inference operates.

Students should compare symbolic reasoning with search, planning, and learning.

---

## Nils J. Nilsson

### Principles of Artificial Intelligence

Role

Theoretical foundation.

Importance

One of the earliest comprehensive treatments of Artificial Intelligence.

Students should pay particular attention to heuristic search and logical
reasoning.

---

# IV. Recommended Reading

---

## Ronald Brachman & Hector Levesque

### Readings in Knowledge Representation

Relationship

Preparation for CS342.

Importance

Demonstrates how logical reasoning becomes organized knowledge.

---

## Peter Jackson

### Introduction to Expert Systems

Relationship

Preparation for CS347.

Importance

Illustrates how formal logic eventually became practical enterprise systems.

---

## Judea Pearl

### Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems

Relationship

Preparation for CS349.

Importance

Introduces reasoning under uncertainty.

Students should compare probabilistic inference with purely logical inference.

---

# Suggested Reading Order

Students new to formal reasoning should read

1. Aristotle
2. Boole
3. Frege
4. Turing
5. Robinson
6. Kowalski
7. Winston
8. Nilsson

The objective is to observe how logical reasoning gradually evolved into modern
Artificial Intelligence.
