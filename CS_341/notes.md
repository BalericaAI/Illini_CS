# Professor's Notes

Many students believe Artificial Intelligence began with computers.

It did not.

Artificial Intelligence began with a much older question.

> How do humans reason?

The first half of this course therefore examines two thousand years of work on
logic before introducing automated reasoning.

Understanding this historical progression allows students to appreciate why
modern AI systems continue to employ ideas first developed by philosophers,
mathematicians, and logicians centuries earlier.

Students should leave this course recognizing that computers did not invent
logic.

They inherited it.
