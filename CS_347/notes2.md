# Professor's Notes

Knowledge-based systems are frequently described as a historical branch of
Artificial Intelligence.

This characterization overlooks their continuing influence.

Modern enterprise AI systems still require

- trusted knowledge,
- explainable reasoning,
- policy enforcement,
- governance,
- auditability,
- and continual knowledge maintenance.

The implementation technologies have changed.

The engineering responsibilities have not.

Throughout this course students should continually ask

> "What knowledge is too important to leave undocumented?"

If an organization cannot answer that question, it has already begun losing
its institutional memory.
