# Professor's Notes

Many students believe that expert systems failed because machine learning
replaced them.

Enterprise experience suggests a different story.

Expert systems continue to exist wherever organizations require transparent
reasoning, regulatory compliance, policy enforcement, and explainable
decision making.

Throughout this course students should continually ask

> "Could another engineer maintain this knowledge twenty years from now?"

If the answer is no, the system has not been engineered successfully.

Knowledge survives organizations only when it has been carefully represented,
documented, validated, and maintained.
