# Recommended Books

Knowledge-Based Programming represents the transition from studying Artificial
Intelligence as a scientific discipline to engineering intelligent systems for
real-world organizations.

Students should recognize that expert systems were not isolated research
projects. They were among the first practical attempts to capture, preserve,
and operationalize human expertise.

The books below illustrate the evolution of Knowledge Engineering from
theoretical concepts to enterprise deployment.

---

# I. Historical Foundations

These works establish the conceptual foundations of Knowledge Engineering.

---

## Edward A. Feigenbaum

### The Fifth Generation

Era

1983

Role

Historical perspective.

Importance

Introduces the vision that knowledge—not merely computation—would define the
next generation of intelligent systems.

Students should compare these expectations with the evolution of modern AI.

Modern Connection

Many ideas regarding knowledge-intensive computing remain relevant within
enterprise AI.

---

## Allen Newell

### Unified Theories of Cognition

Relationship

Understanding expertise.

Importance

Newell examines how intelligent behavior emerges from organized knowledge and
reasoning.

Students should compare cognitive architectures with expert systems.

---

# II. Core Course Texts

---

## Peter Jackson

### Introduction to Expert Systems

Role

Primary course text.

Importance

One of the clearest introductions to expert system architecture.

Topics include

- knowledge bases
- production rules
- inference engines
- certainty factors
- explanation facilities
- knowledge maintenance

Students should understand both the strengths and limitations of expert
systems.

Modern Connection

Rule engines, policy systems, AI governance platforms, and enterprise agents
continue many of these concepts.

---

## Bruce Buchanan & Edward Shortliffe

### Rule-Based Expert Systems

Role

Medical expert systems.

Importance

Documents the development of MYCIN while introducing practical rule-based
reasoning.

Students examine

- rule engineering
- explanation
- certainty factors
- knowledge validation

Modern Connection

Provides one of the earliest examples of trustworthy AI within medicine.

---

## Hayes-Roth, Waterman & Lenat

### Building Expert Systems

Role

Engineering handbook.

Importance

One of the most practical references ever written on constructing complete
knowledge-based systems.

Topics include

- architecture
- implementation
- maintenance
- deployment
- evaluation

Students should compare these engineering concerns with contemporary AI
projects.

---

# III. Knowledge Acquisition

---

## Alison Kidd

### Knowledge Acquisition for Expert Systems:
### A Practical Handbook

Role

Capturing human expertise.

Importance

Demonstrates structured methods for interviewing experts and converting tacit
knowledge into computational models.

Students complete knowledge acquisition exercises throughout the semester.

---

## Hart

### Knowledge Acquisition for Expert Systems

Relationship

Companion reference.

Importance

Provides additional methodologies for eliciting, organizing, and validating
expert knowledge.

---

## Holsapple & Whinston

### Manager's Guide to Expert Systems Using GURU

Role

Enterprise deployment.

Importance

Examines expert systems from the perspective of organizational management,
project planning, and business integration.

Students compare technical success with organizational success.

---

# IV. Knowledge Representation

---

## Ronald Brachman & Hector Levesque

### Readings in Knowledge Representation

Role

Preparation for CS349.

Importance

Provides foundational papers describing how knowledge may be represented,
organized, and reasoned about.

Students should recognize that expert systems depend upon well-designed
knowledge representations.

---

## John Sowa

### Conceptual Structures

Role

Advanced knowledge representation.

Importance

Introduces conceptual graphs and semantic structures for representing complex
knowledge domains.

Students compare conceptual structures with production rules.

---

# V. Advanced Reading

---

## A. Philip Dawid
## Robert G. Cowell
## Steffen L. Lauritzen

### Probabilistic Networks and Expert Systems

Relationship

Bridge toward CS349.

Importance

Demonstrates how probabilistic graphical models extend and complement
classical rule-based expert systems.

Students compare deterministic reasoning with probabilistic inference.

---

## Judea Pearl

### Probabilistic Reasoning in Intelligent Systems

Relationship

Reasoning under uncertainty.

Importance

Introduces Bayesian reasoning as an alternative to certainty factors.

Students should compare Bayesian inference with production rule systems.

---

# Suggested Reading Order

Students unfamiliar with Knowledge Engineering should read

1. Jackson
2. Buchanan & Shortliffe
3. Hayes-Roth
4. Kidd
5. Hart
6. Brachman & Levesque
7. Sowa
8. Pearl
9. Dawid, Cowell & Lauritzen

The objective is to understand how knowledge progresses from acquisition
through representation, reasoning, explanation, and long-term maintenance.
