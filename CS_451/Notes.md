# Professor's Notes

Throughout this curriculum students have repeatedly asked

Can machines reason?

Can machines learn?

Can machines acquire knowledge?

Can machines represent knowledge?

Can machines be trusted?

CS451 asks a different question.

> Can an intelligent system preserve the integrity of its own understanding while continuously changing?

Students should recognize that this question has no final answer.

It is an engineering responsibility rather than an engineering problem.

The objective of this course is therefore not certainty.

The objective is disciplined inquiry.
