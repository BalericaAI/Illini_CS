# Beyond the Books

Books teach principles.

Projects teach implementation.

Conversations teach judgment.

Students are expected to actively participate in

- Weekly Fireside Conversations
- Architecture Reviews
- Whiteboard Design Sessions
- Failure Analysis Discussions
- Research Paper Reviews
- Industry Roundtables

Engineering excellence develops through thoughtful conversation
as much as through technical implementation.

The objective of ECE452 is not merely to graduate capable engineers.

The objective is to graduate engineers capable of leading
the next generation of intelligent enterprises.
