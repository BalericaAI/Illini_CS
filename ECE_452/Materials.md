# ECE452 - Applied Cognitive Engineering
## Recommended Materials

> "Engineering is not the construction of intelligent systems.
> Engineering is the construction of organizations capable of thinking."

---

# Course Philosophy

Unlike previous courses, ECE452 is not organized around algorithms.

It is organized around the engineering of intelligent enterprises.

Students are expected to integrate concepts from Computer Science,
Artificial Intelligence, Enterprise Architecture, Cybernetics,
Knowledge Engineering, Organizational Theory, and Systems Thinking.

The readings below are organized by the engineering questions they help answer.

---

# Professor's Notes

Throughout this curriculum students have learned

- formal reasoning,
- knowledge acquisition,
- pattern recognition,
- expert systems,
- trust,
- neural computation,
- cognition,
- ontology,
- integrity.

ECE452 asks a final question.

> How should these ideas be integrated into organizations
> capable of learning, adapting, and creating value over decades?

Students should leave this course understanding that
technology changes.

Architectures evolve.

Organizations grow.

The responsibility of the engineer is to design systems
that continue to deserve trust long after the original
implementation has been forgotten.
