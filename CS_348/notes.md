# Professor's Notes

These books should not be viewed as obsolete.

Artificial Intelligence did not begin with Large Language Models.

Many of today's AI systems continue to rely upon concepts developed throughout
the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s.

Students are encouraged to ask the following question while reading each book.

> "Which modern AI technology solves the same problem?"

Understanding this continuity is one of the primary goals of the course.
